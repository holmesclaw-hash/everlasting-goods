## Guardrails after approval

1. Store the merchant name, exact destination, tracking URL, approval date, and verification date in the SQLite `affiliate_links` table.
2. Use exact-model product pages only; never substitute merchant search results.
3. Keep `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer"` and a nearby disclosure.
4. Do not copy price, rating, stock, or promotional claims unless an approved current API or merchant feed permits it.
5. Track the destination host and product path without placing private account data in analytics.
6. Compare programs using actual earnings per click; never let commission rate determine editorial ordering.
7. Recheck destinations regularly and remove dead or mismatched variants.

## Application positioning

Use this factual description when an application asks about the site:

> Everlasting Goods is an evidence-tiered repairability and durability database for long-lived tools and shop equipment. Product records identify exact models and cite manufacturer documentation for identity, manuals, parts support, warranty, and current evidence gaps. Commercial links are disclosed and do not determine editorial ordering. The site does not publish fabricated testing, ratings, price, or availability.

Do not claim traffic, subscribers, sales, first-party testing, or audience demographics until connected reporting supports those claims.
