## Durability and maintenance evidence

Victorinox distinguishes honing, which realigns an edge, from sharpening, which removes metal to create a new edge; its current guidance calls for more frequent honing and less frequent sharpening.[15] This supports describing the edge as maintainable, not indestructible.[15]

The current US cutlery warranty describes lifetime coverage for material or manufacturer defects arising during normal use, while requiring proof of purchase and purchase through an authorized Victorinox store or retailer.[17] The article therefore treats the warranty as defect coverage rather than a blanket wear or service-life guarantee.[17]

## Long-term owner evidence
