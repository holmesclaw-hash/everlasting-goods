## Maintenance and repairability boundary

Victorinox distinguishes frequent honing, which realigns the edge with little or no metal removal, from less-frequent sharpening, which removes material to create a new edge.[2] The manufacturer also describes in-store professional sharpening.[2]

This evidence supports classifying edge care as user-serviceable, with a documented professional-service option.[2] It does not establish a model-specific structural repair manual or a replacement-blade, replacement-handle, or field-parts program.[2][1]
