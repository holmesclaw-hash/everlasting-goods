## Decision

Refresh the existing published Estwing B3-3LB guide and structured record rather than create a new article. The route already has an accepted exact-product open-license photograph, and the work protects an existing commercial path.

## Current manufacturer evidence

Estwing’s current product page identifies B3-3LB as the 3-pound member of the drilling-hammer family and documents the one-piece forged construction, 11-inch length, grip, finish, US manufacture, and intended uses.[1]

Estwing’s 2026 catalog separately lists B3-3LB as a US-made drilling hammer with an 11-inch length, 48-ounce head, blue Shock Reduction Grip, smooth faces, UPC 034139620211, and distinct neighboring B3-2LB, B3-4LB, and B3-4LBL variants.[3]

Estwing’s current warranty says the warranty is not a lifetime warranty, covers all-steel hammer failure in normal use subject to exclusions, and says used striking tools cannot be repaired, re-gripped, or refurbished.[2]

## Owner-evidence boundary

A 2023 exact-product discussion supplies fit and workload anecdotes, including favorable comments and a report that the compact 3-pound format felt too heavy for one owner.[4]

A separate 2013 B3-3LB report describes a newly received hammer with a bent head; the owner said Estwing reviewed photographs, sent a replacement, and asked that the original be destroyed.[5] This is one historical claim-handling anecdote, not evidence of a representative defect rate or a guarantee of current warranty handling.

The broader long-term thread contains individual reports of decades with other Estwing hammer models and complaints about shock or vibration, so it supports family context but not an exact-model lifespan estimate.[6]
