- a 120V, single-phase, 60 Hz machine drawing 4.3A on a 15A circuit;
- a 1/2 HP, 3600 RPM capacitor-start induction motor with ball bearings;
- a 4-by-36-inch sanding belt running at 1900 FPM;
- a belt bed that tilts through 90 degrees with a quick-release tension lever and steel platen;
- a 6-inch PSA sanding disc running at 3600 RPM;
- a 6-1/4-by-9-inch work table tilting from 0 to 45 degrees;
- cast-iron base and frame, die-cast aluminum table, and aluminum disc;
- one 2-1/2-inch dust port;
- listed machine weight of 36 pounds;
- manufacture in Thailand and a one-year warranty.

The sheet says it is accurate as of October 20, 2026 and warns that continuing improvements may not describe previously purchased units.
