## Exact identity and current configuration

Grizzly identifies the current item as `G0548ZP`, a single-stage 2 HP canister dust collector with a 240V single-phase motor, one 6-inch main inlet, a three-port 4-inch adapter, a 1-micron canister filter, and a 12-3/4-inch aluminum impeller.[1][2] The current product page reports 1,700 CFM, but this is a manufacturer specification rather than an independent or installed-system measurement.[1]

## Revision and model boundaries

The G0548ZP manual is shared with G1028Z2, G1029Z2, and G1029Z2P, but it marks configuration-specific sections and parts.[3] Sharing a manual does not make these products identical. G1029Z2P, for example, is a 2.5-micron upper-bag collector rather than the G0548ZP 1-micron canister configuration.[19]

The manual includes a 2022 update replacing the inlet adapter on G0548ZP machines manufactured after 2021 with `P0548ZP029V2`; the machine manufacture date must be checked before ordering revision-specific parts.[3][15] G0548Z and G0548 are separate discontinued/superseded product identities.[18][21] Shop Fox W1666 is a separate bag-filter collector with different filtration and impeller details, and South Bend SB1100 is a separate canister product.[20][25]

No specifications, parts claims, owner reports, or commercial identities are transferred among G0548ZP, G0548Z, G0548, G1028Z2, G1029Z2, G1029Z2P, W1666, SB1100, or other related revisions.

## Documentation and serviceability

The owner manual covers assembly, dust-collection system design, operation, canister and collection-bag cleaning/replacement, troubleshooting, wiring diagrams, and illustrated parts.[3] It instructs users to disconnect power before maintenance and directs motor/electrical work to qualified service personnel.[3] This supports bounded owner maintenance and shop serviceability, not classification as a comprehensive step-by-step internal repair manual.

Grizzly publishes both an exact-model online catalog and an illustrated parts list.[4][5] The catalog warns about the post-2021 adapter revision and lists model-specific assemblies. Direct manufacturer order pages were verified for motor `P0548ZP017`, collector body `P0548ZP011`, revised inlet adapter `P0548ZP029V2`, and compatible collection bags `T21821`.[13][14][15][16] Grizzly identifies `H5783` as a compatible replacement canister, but the current orderability of both the canister and the cataloged impeller was not independently established, so the record says parts availability is partial rather than complete or permanent.[17]

Grizzly states that its house-brand products generally have a one-year warranty for the original purchaser and require proof of purchase, subject to published exclusions.[6] Its repair guidance provides technical-support and paid-repair intake and states that parts availability may diminish after a model is discontinued.[7]

## Owner evidence and durability limits

A June 2020 Reddit owner reported owning the G0548ZP for only a few months and described good suction, a minor lower-bag retention annoyance, and a shipped motor that needed alignment.[9] This is a short-duration anecdote, not lifespan evidence.

A September 2013 Sawmill Creek report described fine dust escaping from a newly purchased G0548ZP canister; after support interaction, Grizzly sent a replacement filter.[10] This supports only a bounded new-unit problem and service-response example.

A January 2017 owner published a measured comparison after upgrading from a G0548ZP to a Jet cyclone, including open-inlet and ducted observations, but did not establish the G0548ZP ownership duration or population reliability.[11]
