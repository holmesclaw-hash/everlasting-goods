# Bosch 4100XC-10 structured-record refresh

Reviewed: 2026-09-19

## Identity and package boundary

Bosch identifies 4100XC-10 as a corded 10-inch worksite table saw with a 15-amp motor, 3,650 rpm no-load speed, 30-inch right rip capacity, and an included GTA47W Gravity-Rise wheeled stand.[1]

The manufacturer maps model 4100XC-10 to ten-digit part number 3601L13015, which is the boundary used for parts evidence in this record.[44]

## Manual, parts, service, and warranty

Bosch publishes an exact-model operating and safety manual that covers blade replacement, blade and fence alignment, dust removal, periodic rail and table-lock-cam lubrication, and troubleshooting.[2]

The same manual says electrical or mechanical repairs should be performed by trained repair personnel and recommends service-center inspection and relubrication after six months to one year depending on use.[2]

The manufacturer product page maps 3601L13015 to an official spare-parts catalog and presents a select/order/pay/receive workflow.[44] During this review, the linked U.S. parts endpoint returned a Bosch maintenance notice rather than an inventory page, so individual component availability was not verified.[46]

Bosch’s current corded-tool terms provide a one-year limited warranty to the original purchaser for defects in material or workmanship; misuse, careless handling, unauthorized misrepair, and accessories are excluded, and the complete tool must be returned transportation prepaid for a claim.[4]

## Owner evidence and durability limits

A manufacturer-hosted exact-model review from a self-described professional user reports owning the saw for two to three years: the saw still ran and cut, but the stand tubing collapsed after five or six uses.[44] Bosch’s response on the same page says it changed the tubing and offered the reviewer an appropriate replacement part.[44]

A separate exact-page reviewer later reported that Bosch supplied a stronger replacement leg that was working at the time of the update.[44] These individual reports do not establish a representative failure rate, expected service life, or the configuration of every unit now in commerce.[unverified]

The record therefore keeps expected service life and the buy-or-repair recommendation unverified.[unverified]
