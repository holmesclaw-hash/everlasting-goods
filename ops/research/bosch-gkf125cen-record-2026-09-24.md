# Bosch GKF125CEN structured-record research

Reviewed: 2026-09-24

## Identity and package boundary

Bosch identifies GKF125CEN order number `0601628010` as a 120V corded palm router with a 7.0-amp, 1.25 maximum-horsepower motor, 16,000–35,000 RPM variable speed, a rugged PR101 aluminum fixed base, and a 1/4-inch collet.[1]

The standalone package includes the GKF125CE motor, PR101 fixed base, self-releasing collet, and collet wrench.[1]

The GKF125CEK adds a straight-edge guide and case, while GKF125CEPK adds the PR111 plunge base as well as the guide and case.[1]

Those kit components are not transferred to this standalone record.[unverified]

## Manual, maintenance, and service boundary

Bosch publishes the GKF125CE-family operating and safety manual, and its accessory table explicitly identifies which components are included with GKF125CEN.[2]

The manual tells the owner how to clean the collet and armature-shaft taper, inspect carbon brushes every two to six months, keep vents and switches clean, and avoid disassembly while cleaning.[2]

It recommends Bosch Factory or Authorized Service for all tool service and immediate replacement of noisy bearings.[2]

The manual therefore supports owner maintenance and inspection, not unrestricted internal owner repair.[2]
