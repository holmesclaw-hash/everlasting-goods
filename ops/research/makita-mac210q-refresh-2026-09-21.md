## Manual, parts, maintenance, and warranty

Makita publishes an official manual covering MAC210Q operation, its 50% maximum recommended duty cycle, tank draining, air-filter care, safety-valve checks, yearly check-valve inspection, troubleshooting, and storage.[9]

The manual routes abnormal operation and repair work to authorized service, so it is classified as an operating and maintenance manual rather than a component-repair manual.[9]

Makita’s May 2025 exact-model breakdown identifies part numbers for assemblies and components including the 120V motor, reed-valve head, piston ring, air filter, check valve, switch, capacitor, tank, safety valve, regulator, gauges, coupler, and drain valve.[13] The parts document does not establish that every listed component is currently orderable.[unverified]
