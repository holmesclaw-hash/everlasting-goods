# DEWALT DCS7485B candidate assessment — 2026-09-29

## Verified findings

DEWALT identifies DCS7485B as the US tool-only package for the DCS7485 60V MAX cordless brushless 8-1/4-inch table saw.[1] The package includes the saw, carbide blade, push stick, blade guard, rip fence, non-through-cut riving knife, two blade wrenches, and miter gauge; it does not include a battery or charger.[1]

The DCS7485T1 is a separate kit.[2] It contains the same DCS7485 saw plus one FLEXVOLT battery and a fast charger, so its package evidence is not silently transferred to the DCS7485B record.[2]

The official DCS7485B page publishes a Type 3 manual, while ServiceNet identifies Types 1, 3, and 10.[1][3]

Owners must match the type number on the saw before ordering parts.[3][5]

The Type 10 manual documents weekly air-vent cleaning, dust-door cleanup, and periodic cleaning and lubrication of the height-adjustment screw.[4] It directs repairs, maintenance, brush work, and adjustment beyond those procedures to DEWALT factory or authorized service centers and is not an owner-repair manual.[4]

DEWALT ServiceNet publishes a Type 10 exploded diagram and lists orderable components.[3] This supports partial parts availability, not a claim that every component for every type is continuously orderable.[3][5]

The exact product page states a three-year limited warranty, one-year free service, and a 90-day satisfaction guarantee.[1] Current DEWALT terms limit eligibility and exclude normal wear, abuse, and unauthorized repair, subject to applicable law.[6]

The exact Amazon route identifies DCS7485B as ASIN B01H9BLZ6A, and an independent exact-SKU commercial page corroborates DCS7485B and UPC 885911454162.[7][8] The structured record stores only the exact destination and approved affiliate tag; it makes no price, rating, seller, or availability claim.

Bounded CPSC searches for DCS7485 and DCS7485B did not surface an exact-model recall.[9] A 2008 DEWALT table-saw recall applies to specified DW744 units and must not be attributed to DCS7485B.[10] A negative search is not safety clearance.
