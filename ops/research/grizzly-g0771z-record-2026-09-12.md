# Grizzly G0771Z structured-record research

Reviewed: 2026-09-12

## Identity and durable construction

Grizzly identifies the G0771Z as a 10-inch, 2 HP hybrid table saw that is prewired for 120V and can run on 240V; the current product page documents cast-iron tables and cabinet-mounted cast-iron trunnions.[4]

## Maintenance, parts, and warranty

The current official model-specific manual/update documents table-insert adjustment and says the rest of the owner manual remains applicable; the current official parts-list update documents revision-specific component changes.[6][7]

Grizzly publishes an exact-model online parts catalog with model revisions, replacement identifiers, ordering links, and availability states, while its parts list says not every shown part is guaranteed to remain available.[5][7]

Grizzly's published warranty states that Grizzly products are covered for one year from purchase or shipment for the original purchaser, subject to proof-of-purchase requirements and listed exclusions.[8]

## Owner evidence and limits

One exact-model owner reported that a blade-lift e-clip failed after sawdust and grease had obstructed the mechanism; the owner also reported that the replacement part remained backordered for months.[9]

A second thread initially compared the G0771Z with another Grizzly hybrid saw, but its author later stated that they purchased the G0962A40. The author's two-year follow-up therefore does not describe ownership of the G0771Z and must not be attributed to this record.[11]

The one exact-model report provides limited repair context but does not establish a recurring failure rate, expected service life, or a buy recommendation. Those fields remain not yet verified.

## Commercial destination and image decision

The exact Amazon destination identified ASIN B07K7SZPBR as the Grizzly Industrial G0771Z 10-inch, 2 HP, 120V Hybrid Table Saw with T-Shaped Fence.[12]
