## Maintenance versus internal repair

The shared North American owner manual documents owner-level blade work, dust-bag emptying, guard and laser-lens cleaning, miter and bevel alignment, LS1219L laser-line adjustment, carbon-brush inspection and pair replacement, post-use dust removal, and limited machine-oil lubrication of sliding surfaces.

The manual routes laser-unit failure, defective switch or brake, damaged guard, and all other repairs or undocumented maintenance to Makita authorized or factory service. The exact exploded parts breakdown identifies components, but it is not an internal repair manual and does not provide disassembly order, torque specifications, electrical diagnostics, or component-level repair procedures.
