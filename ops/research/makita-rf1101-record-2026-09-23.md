# Makita RF1101 structured-record research

Reviewed: 2026-09-23

## Identity and package boundary

Makita identifies RF1101 as a corded 11-amp router with variable speed from 8,000 to 24,000 RPM, aluminum motor housing, all-ball-bearing construction, externally accessible brushes, and both 1/4-inch and 1/2-inch collets.[1]

The standalone RF1101 package is kept separate from RF1101KIT2, which adds a plunge base.[1] The standalone product page also contains a conflicting `Base: Plunge` specification line, so that line is not treated as package evidence.[1]

## Manual, parts, service, and warranty

Makita publishes an instruction manual covering RF1101. It documents inspection and replacement of both carbon brushes, while directing other repairs, maintenance, and adjustments to Makita Authorized or Factory Service Centers.[2]

The exact-model parts breakdown identifies the controller, switch, armature, bearings, carbon brushes, collets, base components, cord, and hardware.[3] Several compatible accessories remain linked from the current product page, but current orderability was not established for every repair component; parts availability is therefore `partial`.[1][3]

Makita maintains factory and authorized service routes, including Direct Repair.[5]

The current general policy gives corded products a one-year limited warranty against defects in workmanship and materials and a 30-day return or replacement guarantee, subject to stated exclusions.[4]

## Owner evidence and durability limits

A dated exact-model owner reported using an RF1101 in a duplicator for several years without problems.[6] A separate owner reported that an RF1101 used heavily in a router table developed speed surging after seven years and was considering controller replacement rather than immediate disposal.[7]

These reports support only anecdotal context. They do not establish a representative failure rate or expected service life, so lifespan and the buy or repair recommendation remain unverified.[unverified]

A seven-year Reddit report surfaced during research, but its thread concerned RP2303 and the duration statement did not establish that it referred to RF1101; it is excluded from the record.[unverified]

## Safety and image limits

The official manual contains current operating, grounding, router, and maintenance safety instructions.[2] A bounded CPSC search did not establish exact-model recall applicability and is not treated as safety clearance.[unverified]
