## Editorial angle

“Is a Moccamaster Buy It for Life? K741.64B and KBGV Evidence Review.”

The historical K741.64B and current KBGV Select are explicitly treated as different models. Evidence about the photographed machine does not establish the lifespan of a current brewer.

## Claim-level evidence

The Commons record identifies the photographed exact model as a 15-year-old Technivorm Moccamaster K741.64B with a replacement water container.[5]

Moccamaster’s current KBGV page documents a 1.25-liter / 40-ounce brewer, automatic drip-stop basket, metal and specified food-safe plastic construction, and a 100-minute automatic shutoff; the page also directs owners to descale every 100 cycles or at least every three months.[1]

The current KBGV Select parts catalog says removable pieces are replaceable and that parts remain available for some older brewers no longer sold.[2]

Moccamaster USA currently describes a flat-rate factory repair service including parts, labor, shipping, and a one-year warranty on completed repair work.[3]

The current US warranty gives a new standard brewer five years of defect coverage, excludes the carafe and parts, and excludes problems tied to misuse, neglected descaling, modification, or unauthorized repair.[4]

A Reddit owner report describes an inherited Moccamaster believed to date from the late 1980s or early 1990s and still used daily; replies report other machines approaching or exceeding two decades.[6]

A 2024 BestReviews Testing Lab review praised KBGV Select coffee and brew speed but criticized loose-fitting components, carafe handling and dripping, continued basket dripping, and the lack of programming features.[37]

The identity-checked merchant destination is the matte-black model 53948 KBGV Select, ASIN B093DXS54M; no price, rating, seller, or availability value is republished.[8]
