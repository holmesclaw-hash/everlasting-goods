## Exact identity and package boundary

Makita identifies GFD01Z as the U.S. 40V max XGT brushless 1/2-inch driver-drill, tool only.[1]

The official page is marked `DISCONTINUED` and identifies GFD03Z as the replacement model.[1]

The GFD01 manual documents a 40V max driver-drill with a 7-1/8-inch overall length, 0-650 and 0-2,600 RPM no-load speeds, drilling capacities up to 13/16 inch in steel, and listed BL4025 or BL4040 battery compatibility with the DC40RA charger.[2]
