# Makita RT0701C structured-record refresh

Reviewed: 2026-10-22

## Identity and package boundary

Makita identifies RT0701C as a corded fixed-base compact router with a 6.5A motor, 10,000–30,000 RPM variable speed, electronic speed control, soft start, a heavy-duty aluminum motor housing, and a 1/4-inch collet.[1]

The U.S. RT0701C package includes one trimmer base, one 1/4-inch collet, one straight guide, and two spanner wrenches; it is not the RT0701CX3 multi-base kit.[1]

Amazon ASIN B00E7D3V4S currently identifies model RT0701C and the fixed-base package.[6]

## Maintenance, components, and service

The joint RT0700C/RT0701C instruction manual covers operation, overload boundaries, inspection, paired carbon-brush replacement, and service routing, but it does not provide component-level diagnosis or internal repair procedures.[2]

Makita’s illustrated breakdown identifies the controller, switch, field, armature, bearings, carbon brushes, cord, housing, collet components, and base components by part number.[3]

The manufacturer’s parts list effective October 15, 2026 carries RT0701C armature, controller, field, housing, and bearing entries, plus the shared switch, cord, carbon-brush set, collet nut, and related base components.[7] Some older breakdown numbers are marked discontinued or point to alternates, and list presence does not prove current on-hand inventory or indefinite future supply.[7]

Makita offers factory and authorized service-center routing plus a direct-repair path; the manual directs repairs, other maintenance, and adjustment to those service channels using Makita replacement parts.[2][5]

## Warranty boundary

Because RT0701C is corded, Makita’s one-year general product limited warranty applies rather than the separate three-year lithium-ion-tool term; the policy also publishes a 30-day return or replacement guarantee.[4]

Published exclusions include repairs attempted by others, normal wear, abuse, misuse, improper maintenance, and alterations.[4]

## Owner evidence and durability limits

On the exact-ASIN page, one verified-purchase reviewer dated July 7, 2023 reports running RT0701C in a garage CNC for 5–9 hours at a time and buying replacement brushes that had not yet been needed, but the review states only that the tool had been owned “for a while.”[6]

The same exact-ASIN page exposes mixed durability excerpts, including both a claimed under-five-hour burnout and a commercial cabinet-shop durability claim; these are individual reports without controlled usage or representative sampling.[6]

A professional woodworker’s January 16, 2025 review of the jointly documented RT0700C sibling model reports at least six years and an estimated 1,500 hard hours of hardwood use.[8] That is useful family evidence, but it is not exact-U.S.-SKU lifespan evidence for RT0701C.[unverified]

Expected service life, representative failure rate, annual maintenance cost, repair economics, and a buy-or-repair recommendation therefore remain not yet verified.[unverified]
