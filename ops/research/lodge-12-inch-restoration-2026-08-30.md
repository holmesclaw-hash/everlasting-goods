## Exact product and destination

Lodge’s current Classic Cast Iron Skillet product family offers a 12-inch size, seasons the line with natural vegetable oil, and documents compatibility with induction and other stovetops, ovens, grills, and campfires.[21]

The exact Amazon product-detail page at ASIN `B00006JSUB` identifies the standalone Lodge 12-inch seasoned cast-iron skillet.[47] The similarly named `B00006JSUA` is the 10.25-inch variant and is not the destination used for this guide.[46] No merchant price, rating, seller, or availability value is copied to Everlasting Goods.

## Durability, maintenance, and warranty evidence

Lodge describes seasoning as polymerized oil and publishes a complete wash, oil, bake, and cool process for rebuilding it.[24] Lodge’s current cleaning instructions direct owners to wash, dry completely, and oil the pan; the page also documents a rust-recovery path and warns against soaking or dishwasher use.[23]

Lodge’s Made Right limited lifetime warranty covers specified cracks, warping, shipping damage, and casting defects during normal household use, while excluding rust, pitting, roughness, sticky or flaking seasoning, misuse, drops, overheating, and commercial use.[22]

A detailed independent review identifies the exact 10SK/L10SK3 12-inch family and documents high mass, slow preheating, seasoning upkeep, a rough surface, and uneven stovetop heating as material ownership tradeoffs.[29]
