## Verified findings

Makita identifies MAC5200 as its United States 120V, 3.0 maximum-horsepower Big Bore air compressor with an oil-lubricated cast-iron pump, 5.2-gallon tank, 6.5 CFM at 90 PSI, 140 PSI cut-out pressure, roll cage, folding handle, wheels, and two 1/4-inch universal quick couplers.[1][2] The owner manual separately lists 2.1 running horsepower, 13.8 amps at maximum pressure, and a recommended maximum 50 percent duty cycle.[2]
