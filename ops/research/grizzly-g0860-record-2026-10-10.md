# Grizzly G0860 structured-record research

Reviewed: 2026-10-10

## Identity and configuration boundary

Grizzly currently identifies G0860 as a portable two-stage cyclone dust collector with a 1-1/2 HP, 110V single-phase 15A motor, 20-gallon collection drum, 6-inch inlet, two-port 4-inch adapter, 1-micron canister filter, and wireless remote.[1][13]

The exact record is limited to G0860. The related G0861 is a 2 HP, 220V machine and G0862 is a 3 HP, 220V machine. Their specifications, ownership reports, parts, and commercial identities are not transferred to G0860.[2]

## Maintenance, repair, and parts boundaries

The official owner manual covers assembly, operation, filter and collection-bag procedures, periodic maintenance, troubleshooting, wiring diagrams, and illustrated parts. It does not provide step-by-step internal component-repair procedures, and it reserves specified electrical work for a qualified electrician or service person. The file also carries a later G0860/G0861 update for machines manufactured since January 2024, so owners must match documentation and parts to their machine revision.[2]

Grizzly publishes an exact-model online parts catalog and illustrated parts list. Current manufacturer pages were found for the replacement canister filter, motor, circuit board, impeller, and remote control.[3][4][5]

The parts list expressly says not all parts may be available and availability can change. This supports a conservative “partial” parts rating, not a promise that every component remains orderable.[4]

Grizzly says it can repair most Grizzly-brand items at its Washington or Missouri locations, requires customer contact before shipment, and provides repair estimates after inspection.[7]

The resulting serviceability classification is shop-serviceable: owners have documented filter, bag, and maintenance tasks, while internal electrical diagnosis or repair may require a qualified person. The owner manual is not classified as an internal repair manual.[2][7]

## Warranty boundary

Grizzly’s published house-brand warranty covers the original purchaser for one year from purchase or shipment against defects in material and workmanship, subject to proof-of-purchase requirements. The terms exclude, among other causes, unauthorized repairs or alterations, misuse, improper conditions, lack of maintenance, non-Grizzly parts, normal wear, and consumable parts.[6]

## Owner evidence and durability limits

A January 2021 Fine Woodworking hands-on review reports that the reviewer assembled and tested G0860 and then bought the unit. The review is positive but does not establish a long ownership duration or later condition.[8]

A June 2020 LumberJocks owner report says the writer bought G0860 the prior year and experienced repeated breaker trips, a replacement motor that did not resolve the issue, and dissatisfaction with support. This is one negative anecdote after roughly one year, not a representative failure rate.[9]

The two reports are mixed, self-selected, and too limited to establish expected service life, population-level failure rate, repair economics, or a repair-versus-replace recommendation. Those fields remain not yet verified.

## Recall-search boundary

A bounded model-number search did not surface G0860 on Grizzly’s recalled-items page or as a matching CPSC recall result on October 10, 2026.[11][12] This is a documented no-match search, not safety clearance.

## Commercial destination

Amazon ASIN B07K1YTJZD resolved to a page titled for the Grizzly Industrial G0860 1-1/2 HP portable cyclone dust collector.[10] The record uses that exact destination with the approved affiliate tag and publishes no Amazon price, rating, seller, or availability.

## Image and article decision

No lawfully reusable exact-G0860 photograph was accepted. Searches of Wikimedia Commons, Flickr, and Openverse did not produce a source whose exact-model identity and commercial reuse rights could be verified. Grizzly, retailer, and editorial-review images are publicly viewable but no reuse permission was established.

The structured record therefore remains text-only. No article was drafted or published because the exact-product photo gate is not satisfied and the owner evidence does not support a Buy-It-for-Life conclusion.

## Publication decision

G0860 qualifies for a conservative T2 structured record because official sources establish exact identity, configuration, maintenance and service scope, parts documentation, warranty, and repair routing.[1][2][3][4][6][7]

The verdict remains “not yet verified.” Parts are rated partial, serviceability is bounded to documented owner maintenance plus qualified repair, and all lifespan, representative failure-rate, repair-economics, and recommendation fields remain unverified.
