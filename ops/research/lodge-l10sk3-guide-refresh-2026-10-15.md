# Lodge L10SK3 cast-iron guide refresh

Reviewed: 2026-10-15  
Status: implemented and live-verified; owner-report wording corrected during independent review

## Current manufacturer facts

Lodge’s current 12-inch Classic Cast Iron Skillet variant is made in the USA, seasoned with vegetable oil, compatible with induction and other stovetops, ovens, grills, and campfires, and listed at 18 inches long, 12.56 inches wide, 2.25 inches high, and 7.69 pounds.[1]

Lodge’s seasoning instructions document a wash, thin-oil, bake, and cool process for rebuilding the cooking surface.[4] Its cleaning guidance directs owners to wash, dry completely, and oil the pan, and it documents home rust recovery.[3]

The Made Right limited lifetime warranty covers specified cracks, warping, shipping damage, and casting defects during normal household use while excluding rust, pitting, seasoning condition, misuse, overheating, drops, commercial use, and care inconsistent with Lodge’s instructions.[2]

## Owner evidence and limits

The exact-family independent review documents high mass, slow preheating, a rough factory surface, seasoning upkeep, and uneven stovetop heating as ownership tradeoffs.[5] A Permies participant reported several years with Lodge cookware that included a 12-inch skillet.[8] A separate 2020 owner post says the owner bought a 12-inch Lodge about 12 years earlier and used it for most meals cooked at home; it does not establish the current SKU.[6]

These individual reports do not establish a representative expected service life, failure rate, annual maintenance cost, or repair-versus-replace threshold. The refreshed guide keeps those limits explicit.
