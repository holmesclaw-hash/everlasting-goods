## Maintenance, warranty, and repair boundary

Lodge documents routine cleaning as washing, drying thoroughly, and applying a thin oil layer; its guidance also provides a rust-recovery path.[6] Lodge separately documents a complete wash, oil, bake, and cool process for rebuilding seasoning.[7]

Lodge’s Made Right limited lifetime warranty lists cracks, warping, specified casting defects, and shipping damage among covered conditions, while excluding rust, pitting, roughness, sticky or flaking seasoning, misuse, overheating, drops, commercial use, and care inconsistent with the instructions.[5]

The skillet is a solid cast-iron body with no documented replaceable assemblies.[4] Surface rust and seasoning wear are user-maintainable under Lodge’s guidance, but structural cracks and warping are not documented user repairs.[5][6]
