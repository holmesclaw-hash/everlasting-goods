## Maintenance and repair boundary

The exact North American manual documents:

- blade installation and removal;
- blade-guard cleaning without removing or defeating the guard spring;
- kerf-board positioning;
- maximum-cutting-capacity, fence, miter, and bevel alignment;
- regular carbon-brush inspection and paired replacement;
- post-use dust removal and machine-oil lubrication of sliding portions.

The same manual routes blade-guard replacement to Makita service, routes a malfunctioning electric brake to a Makita service center, and states that repairs or maintenance and adjustment beyond the described work should be performed by Makita Authorized or Factory Service Centers. This supports `user-serviceable` for the documented owner tasks, not a claim that internal electrical, brake, switch, guard, controller, or motor repairs are owner procedures. The owner manual is not classified as a complete internal repair manual.
