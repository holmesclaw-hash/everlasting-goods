## Insert conflict and safety boundary

Current official sources do not agree cleanly about the inserts:

- The product page and current specification sheet identify 12 two-sided 14 x 14 x 2 mm HSS inserts.
- The current manual's G0946 parts list labels P0946160-2 as a 14 x 14 x 2 mm carbide insert.
- Grizzly's T32739 page identifies 14.25 x 14.25 x 2 mm two-sided carbide inserts as G0946-compatible and sells them in a ten-pack.
- The G0946 product page says the T32739 inserts are compatible but then says “all 26” must be replaced even though the same page lists 12 inserts. Twenty-six is not accepted as the G0946 cutter count.
- The current G0946 parts page maps P0946160-2 to “Now: T32937,” which it describes as a six-inch saw-blade/scoring product rather than an insert.

The record preserves these conflicts. It does not advise buying a full replacement set or mixing insert dimensions without confirmation from Grizzly. The manual does document individual insert rotation and replacement, a 48–50 inch-pound screw torque, and cleaning and seating requirements.
