# Makita XCV11Z structured-record research

Reviewed: 2026-09-24

## Identity and package boundary

Makita identifies XCV11Z as an 18V LXT brushless cordless two-gallon HEPA portable wet/dry dust extractor and vacuum sold as a tool-only package.[1]

Makita specifies 57 CFM, 27 inches of water lift, and a package containing a flexible hose, wide nozzle, crevice nozzle, HEPA filter, and pre-filter; battery, charger, and shoulder strap are not included.[1]

The related XCV11T is a kit with two batteries, and evidence from that package or the ADCV11Z Outdoor Adventure variant is not transferred to this record.[1]

## Manual, parts, service, and warranty

Makita publishes an exact XCV11 operating, safety, and maintenance manual.[2] It instructs the owner to empty the tank, remove and clean the HEPA filter, occasionally wash it with water, dry it thoroughly in the shade, and replace worn filters.[2]

The manual directs repairs and other maintenance or adjustment to Makita Authorized or Factory Service Centers, so it is not classified as an owner-repair manual.[2]

Makita publishes an exact XCV11Z exploded diagram and parts list with numbered components including the switch, controller, stator, rotor, fan, seals, filter, tank, hose, and nozzles.[3]

The product page identifies current filter and accessory part numbers, but individual internal repair-part orderability was not verified; parts support is therefore classified `partial`.[1][3]

Makita currently warrants lithium-ion tools against defects in workmanship and materials for three years from original purchase, subject to its published limitations and exclusions.[4] Makita also maintains factory, authorized, and direct-repair service paths.[5]

## Owner evidence and durability limits

In one exact-model discussion, an owner reports six years of frequent use and good collection with a cordless planer while acknowledging limited extraction from a miter saw.[6] Another participant reports years of hard use without complaint, while the thread author reports returning the XCV11Z immediately because suction was lower than expected.[6]

These reports are conflicting anecdotes rather than representative measurements. Expected service life and the buy or repair recommendation remain unverified.[unverified]

## Commercial destination and image limit

Amazon ASIN `B07M8JTDNS` identifies the exact XCV11Z tool-only package.[7] The database stores only the exact tagged destination and does not publish Amazon price, rating, seller, or availability.[7]

No article is created, and the structured record carries no image because reusable exact-product image rights were not established in this research.[unverified]

## Publication decision

Exact manufacturer identity, owner-maintenance instructions, an exact numbered parts breakdown, current service routes, current warranty terms, and qualified mixed owner evidence justify a conservative T2 structured record.[1][2][3]

The warranty and repair routes complete the support boundary.[4][5]

The record leaves individual internal-part orderability, representative service life, and the buy or repair recommendation unverified.[unverified]
